ARTICLE III — MANAGEMENT


3.1 Member-Managed

The Company shall be member-managed. The Member has full authority to manage the business and affairs of the Company.


3.2 Powers of the Member

The Member shall have the authority to:

  • Enter into contracts and agreements on behalf of the Company
  • Open and manage bank accounts
  • Hire employees and independent contractors
  • Acquire, hold, and dispose of property
  • Borrow money and incur obligations
  • Execute any documents necessary for the Company's business
  • Make all decisions regarding the Company's operations

3.3 Title

The Member may use the title Managing Member, CEO, Founder, or any other title appropriate for conducting business.



ARTICLE IV — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS


4.1 Initial Contribution

The Member has contributed the following to the Company as an initial capital contribution:


  • Cash: $0
  • Other property/services: None

4.2 Additional Contributions

The Member is not required to make additional capital contributions but may do so at any time.


4.3 No Interest on Contributions

No interest shall be paid on any capital contributions.



ARTICLE V — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS


5.1 Profits and Losses

All profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated to the Member.


5.2 Distributions

The Member may make distributions from the Company at any time and in any amount, provided that such distributions do not render the Company unable to pay its debts as they become due in the ordinary course of business.


5.3 Tax Treatment

The Company shall be treated as a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes. All income, losses, deductions, and credits shall be reported on the Member's personal tax return (Schedule C or equivalent).



ARTICLE VI — BANK ACCOUNTS AND RECORDS


6.1 Bank Accounts

The Company shall maintain one or more bank accounts in the name of the Company. Only the Member (or persons authorized by the Member) may sign on Company accounts.


6.2 Separation of Funds

The Member shall maintain the Company's funds separate from personal funds. Company funds shall not be commingled with the personal funds of the Member.


6.3 Records

The Company shall maintain the following records at its principal office:

  • This Operating Agreement and any amendments
  • Articles of Organization and any amendments
  • Federal, state, and local tax returns for the most recent three years
  • Financial statements for the most recent three years
  • A current list of the Member's name and address


ARTICLE VII — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION


7.1 Limited Liability

The Member shall not be personally liable for the debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a Member.


7.2 Indemnification

The Company shall indemnify the Member against any losses, damages, or expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) incurred in connection with the Company's business, provided the Member acted in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interest of the Company.



ARTICLE VIII — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST


8.1 Transfers

The Member may freely transfer, sell, or assign all or any portion of the membership interest in the Company.


8.2 Effect of Transfer

A transfer of the entire membership interest shall make the transferee the sole member of the Company, and this Agreement shall be amended accordingly.



ARTICLE IX — DISSOLUTION


9.1 Events of Dissolution

The Company shall be dissolved upon the earliest of:

  • The written decision of the Member to dissolve
  • The sale or disposition of all Company assets
  • Entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under Utah law
  • Any event that makes it unlawful for the Company to continue

9.2 Winding Up

Upon dissolution, the Member shall wind up the Company's affairs by:

1. Paying all debts and obligations of the Company

2. Distributing any remaining assets to the Member


9.3 Articles of Dissolution

Upon completion of winding up, the Member shall file Articles of Dissolution with the Utah Division of Corporations.



ARTICLE X — GENERAL PROVISIONS


10.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Utah.


10.2 Amendments

This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Member.


10.3 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.


10.4 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement regarding the organization and operation of the Company and supersedes any prior agreements.
