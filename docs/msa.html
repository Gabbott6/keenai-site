1. SERVICES


Provider will perform AI consulting, software development, and implementation services ("Services") as described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement. Each SOW is incorporated by reference into this Agreement.


2. STATEMENTS OF WORK


Each SOW will specify:

  • Description of Services and deliverables
  • Timeline and milestones
  • Fees and payment schedule
  • Acceptance criteria

In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, the SOW controls for that specific engagement.


3. FEES & PAYMENT


3.1 Fees

Client agrees to pay Provider the fees set forth in each SOW.


3.2 Payment Schedule

Unless otherwise specified in a SOW:

  • 50% of the project fee is due upon execution of the SOW ("Deposit")
  • 50% of the project fee is due upon delivery of the final deliverables

3.3 Monthly Retainer

If applicable, retainer fees are invoiced on the 1st of each month and due within 15 days (Net 15).


3.4 Late Payment

Invoices unpaid after 15 days past due will incur a late fee of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.


3.5 Expenses

Pre-approved expenses (hosting, third-party APIs, software licenses) will be invoiced at cost with no markup.


4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY


4.1 Provider IP

Provider retains all rights to its pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, and methodologies ("Provider IP"), including any general-purpose AI models, templates, or reusable components developed before or during the engagement.


4.2 Client Deliverables

Upon full payment, Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, modify, and deploy all custom deliverables created specifically for Client under a SOW ("Client Deliverables").


4.3 Client Data

All Client data, business information, and proprietary content remain the exclusive property of Client. Provider will not use Client data for any purpose other than performing the Services.


4.4 No Resale Restriction

Provider may create similar solutions for other clients in different markets or geographies, provided no Client Confidential Information is used or disclosed.


5. CONFIDENTIALITY


5.1 Definition

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party, including business plans, customer data, financial information, software code, and trade secrets.


5.2 Obligations

Each party agrees to:

  • Protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the same care it uses for its own
  • Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent
  • Use Confidential Information only for purposes of this Agreement

5.3 Exceptions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

  • Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
  • Was known to the receiving party before disclosure
  • Is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's information
  • Is required to be disclosed by law or court order

5.4 Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.


6. WARRANTIES & REPRESENTATIONS


6.1 Provider Warranties

Provider warrants that:

  • Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner
  • Deliverables will materially conform to the specifications in the applicable SOW for a period of 30 days after delivery ("Warranty Period")
  • Provider has the right to enter into this Agreement and perform the Services

6.2 Warranty Remedy

During the Warranty Period, Provider will correct any material defects in the deliverables at no additional cost to Client.


6.3 Disclaimer

EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH ABOVE, PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.


7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY


7.1 Cap

Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client under the applicable SOW giving rise to the claim.


7.2 Exclusion

IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS OR REVENUE, REGARDLESS OF THE THEORY OF LIABILITY.


8. INDEMNIFICATION


8.1 By Provider

Provider will indemnify Client against third-party claims arising from Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services.


8.2 By Client

Client will indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Client's use of the deliverables, Client's data, or Client's violation of applicable laws.


9. TERM & TERMINATION


9.1 Term

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues until terminated.


9.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW with 30 days' written notice.


9.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party:

  • Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice
  • Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

9.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:

  • Client pays for all Services performed and expenses incurred through the termination date
  • Provider delivers all completed and in-progress work product
  • Both parties return or destroy the other's Confidential Information

9.5 Survival

Sections 4 (IP), 5 (Confidentiality), 6 (Warranties), 7 (Limitation of Liability), and 8 (Indemnification) survive termination.


10. GENERAL PROVISIONS


10.1 Independent Contractor

Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship.


10.2 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Utah, without regard to conflict of laws principles.


10.3 Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising under this Agreement will first be subject to good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, disputes will be resolved by binding arbitration in Utah County, Utah under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.


10.4 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with all SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.


10.5 Amendments

This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.


10.6 Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except in connection with a merger or acquisition.


10.7 Notices

All notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses listed above (email is acceptable).
